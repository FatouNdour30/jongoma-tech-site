Jongoma Tech is a Senegalese technology startup, founded and led by women. We design digital solutions for organisations, and we put them to work for women and their families.

Jongoma

Our name

In Wolof, “jongoma” means a woman in all her dignity and elegance.

The name says who we are: a 100% women-led startup, founded by women, working for women. From the field to management, our team is entirely female.

Mission

Solving real problems

Start from a specific problem in our society, and answer it with fieldwork, technology and proof.

Vision

Technology that leaves no one behind

Tools designed for all women, including those who read little, speak Wolof or only have a basic phone.

Values

The problem

First affected, last served

Women left out

Services, financial products and digital tools are rarely designed for them, or in their language.

Aid that gets lost

Part of the aid and of the money sent home never reaches the people it is meant for.

Programmes without proof

Many social projects cannot show what they have really changed.

Missing data

Decisions are made without knowing women's reality, because they are rarely surveyed.

The language barrier

Women who read little or mostly speak Wolof are shut out of many digital services.

Untapped talent

Young women graduates remain unemployed while companies look for digital profiles.
