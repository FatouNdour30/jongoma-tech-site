01 · Client area

From your question to a proven result

Every request follows the same path, whatever the service. You always know where your file stands, and who is handling it.

Request in 3 steps

Choose a service (or “I don't know yet”), describe your need, attach photos or PDFs.

Step-by-step tracking

Received, assigned, quote, mission, result: every step is dated and visible.

Messaging with your manager

Ask your questions in each file, with attachments.

Quote and payment

Accept the quote, pay by Wave or Orange Money, get a printable receipt.

Field evidence

Time-stamped photos, GPS location on the map, scanned receipts, final report.

Notifications

A new status, a message, a quote: you are notified right away.
